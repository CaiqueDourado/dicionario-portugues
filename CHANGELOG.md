# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-30

- 272,626 words with rule-based fields (accents removed, stem, reversed word, vowels, consonants, Metaphone, fingerprint, n-grams) and `ocorrencias`.
- 2,916,780 AI texts in 19 fields (`text-davinci-002` and `gpt-3.5-turbo`, 2022–2023, not reviewed), each with its model; davinci cells that copied the ChatGPT text removed.
- 2,287,766 rhymes and 11,868,060 same-key pairs.
- Cleaning: prompt echoes, broken phonetic transcriptions, texts where the model talks about itself and texts with lost characters; `palavra_sem_acento`, `ao_contrario` and `numero_letras` recomputed.
